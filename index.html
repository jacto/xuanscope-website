FAQ

Does my second phone need the app installed?
Yes — install XuanScope on each phone you want to use as a camera. The director phone shows a QR code; the camera phone scans it and connects directly over your local Wi-Fi.
Which USB cameras are supported?
Standard UVC devices — webcams and capture cards — work over a USB OTG adapter. Multiple cameras can be mixed depending on your plan.
Where can I stream to?
Anywhere that accepts an RTMP stream: YouTube Live, Twitch, Facebook Live, or your own media server. You can also record locally to MP4 without streaming.
Does my video go through your servers?
No. XuanScope works entirely on your devices — camera links stay on your local Wi-Fi, and streaming goes directly to the RTMP server you configure. We don't run any media servers. See our Privacy Policy.
Which Android version do I need?
Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer.
