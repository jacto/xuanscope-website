Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XuanScope ("App"), developed and operated by Jietao Huang (developer account "JIETAO", "we", "us", "Developer"), handles information when you use the App.

In short: the App works entirely on your device. We do not operate any server that receives your personal data, we do not collect analytics or advertising identifiers, and we do not share any data with third parties for commercial purposes.

1. Information Processed Only on Your Device

The following data is captured, processed, and stored exclusively on your device. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

DataPurposeWhere it stays
Camera preview and USB (UVC) camera videoMulti-camera directing and compositionDevice only
Microphone audioAudio mixing, noise suppression, and recordingDevice only
Images and videos you importUse as picture sources in your compositionDevice only
Recordings (MP4) and photos (JPEG) you createSaved by your own actionYour device's media storage
App settings (layouts, overlays, shortcuts, streaming URLs)Remember your configurationDevice local storage
Diagnostic logs (crash info, device model, OS version)Troubleshooting problems you reportDevice local storage; see Section 4

You can delete all of this at any time by deleting the files in your gallery or file manager, or by uninstalling the App.

2. Information Transmitted Only Within Your Local Network

When you use the "phone source" feature to connect another phone on your Wi-Fi network:

  • The connecting device's name and model are exchanged between your own devices on the local network so they can find and identify each other;
  • The phone's camera or screen video and audio are transmitted directly between your devices over the local network for composition.

This traffic never passes through, and is not accessible to, any server of ours — we do not operate one. Security note: these features rely on general-purpose network protocols without end-to-end encryption or strong peer authentication. Use them only on networks you trust, and see our Terms of Service for details.

3. Subscriptions and Payments (Processed by Google, Not Us)

If you purchase a subscription, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment method, your billing address, or your Google account credentials. The App only checks, locally on your device, whether a valid subscription is associated with your Google Play account.

Google's handling of your payment and account data is described in Google's own policies:

  • Google Payments Privacy Notice: payments.google.com
  • Google Play Terms of Service: play.google.com

4. Information We Receive Only If You Contact Us

If you contact us for support at hjacto@gmail.com (for example, by using the "export diagnostic log" feature and emailing it), we receive the content you choose to send — typically your message and, if you attach it, a diagnostic log containing device model, Android version, app version, and crash details. We use it solely to diagnose and resolve your reported issue, keep it only as long as needed for that purpose, and do not add it to any marketing or profiling system.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing — used for subscription purchases (see Section 3).
  • The App contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no tracking SDKs, and no social sharing SDKs.
  • The open source components used in the App are listed in the App's "Open Source Licenses" page; none of them transmits data to us.

6. Data Security

All processing happens on your device or between your own devices on your local network, which limits exposure by design. Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no developer-held database that could be breached. Local network transmissions are not end-to-end encrypted; please use trusted networks.

7. Children

The App is a professional directing tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not maintain any server-side store of your personal data:

  • Access / deletion: everything the App stores is on your device and deletable by you at any time (recordings via your gallery; settings and logs via uninstalling the App).
  • Requests to us: if you contact us (Section 4), you may ask us to delete the correspondence at any time by emailing hjacto@gmail.com.
  • Google Play data: requests regarding your payment and subscription records should be directed to Google (support.google.com/googleplay).
  • Regional rights: to the extent you are located in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California, or another jurisdiction with statutory privacy rights (e.g., GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection; CCPA/CPRA rights to know and opt out of "sale/sharing"), you can exercise those rights using the methods above. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

9. Data Retention

We retain support correspondence (Section 4) only as long as necessary to resolve your issue. The App itself retains nothing outside your device.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. The current version is always available in the App, and the "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Material changes will be highlighted in the App.
